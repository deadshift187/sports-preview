Privacy

Effective 2026-10-15 - early tester build.

No ads. No tracking. No account.

Your choices - the services you have, teams and sports you follow, your city, and any bets you add - are stored only on your own device (in your browser's local storage). They are never sent to us or anyone else. There are no analytics, no trackers, no cookies for advertising, and no third-party scripts collecting your activity.

Where the data comes from

Game schedules and where-to-watch/listen come from public sources (ESPN, TheSportsDB and sourced broadcast listings). Tapping a watch or listen option opens that service's own app or website; we never play streams and never handle your logins or passwords.

Location

"Use my location" is off unless you turn it on, and even then it only sets your time zone, on your device. Your location is never sent anywhere.

Bets

My Bets only shows bets you entered or imported from a public source, and links out to the book or market. It never places, changes or settles a bet, never handles money, and never asks for a password or an API key. Betting links carry no affiliate codes.

Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control

We don't collect analytics in the first place, so there is nothing to opt out of - a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal changes nothing because we never track you either way.

Deleting your data

Open Setup and tap Reset app & clear my data to erase everything this app stored on your device. Clearing your browser's site data for this page does the same thing.
