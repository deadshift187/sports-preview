Privacy

No ads. No tracking. This is an early tester build.

Your choices - the services you have, teams and sports you follow, your city, and any bets you add - are stored only on your own device (in your browser). They are never sent to us or anyone else, and there are no analytics or trackers.

Game schedules and where-to-watch/listen come from public sources (ESPN, TheSportsDB and sourced broadcast listings). Tapping a watch or listen option opens that service's own app or website; we never play streams or handle your logins.

"Use my location" is off unless you turn it on, and even then it only sets your time zone on your device.
